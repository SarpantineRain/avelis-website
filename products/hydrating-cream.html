Materials and design notes

A single form that moves from circle to line.

The body begins as a 35 mm circle and flattens into a straight sealed edge. Its perimeter stays the same along the whole length, so the change reads as one continuous gesture.

The frosted cap carries 48 vertical ribs, which break the light into fine lines at the base.

The label is kept to the wordmark, a single rule, the product name and the volume.

Design notes describe the 3D object. All descriptions are conceptual creative copy, not verified cosmetic performance claims.

Materials and design notes

Weight in the glass, stillness in the form.

Heavy glass gives the jar its presence: a 9 mm wall, a 14 mm base and a side that swells very slightly.

A metal rim follows the slope of the shoulder, and the lid carries two fine grooves.

The balm inside was shaped as a single swirl, with its peak set slightly off centre.

Design notes describe the 3D object. All descriptions are conceptual creative copy, not verified cosmetic performance claims.

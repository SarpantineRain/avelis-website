Materials and design notes

Eight softened faces around one note of red.

The case is a soft octagon: its corners are rounded just enough for light to travel around them without losing the edge.

A metal collar with a fine groove marks the point where the bullet rises.

Inside, two full turns of a spiral raise the bullet by 22 mm. The mechanism was modelled in full, although it stays hidden.

Design notes describe the 3D object. All descriptions are conceptual creative copy, not verified cosmetic performance claims.
